Why this model?

Learned-assisted numerical IK plus checked direct/RRT* planning had the highest success and lowest planning time in the 10,000 fixed-target comparison. That comparison had no external blocks. Mouse tracking defaults to learned IK + classical RRT* with continuous checked curves; physical accuracy depends on arm calibration.

Choose a target preset or copy the live flange pose, then plan and preview the entire path. The selected fixed-target planner generates a checked trajectory; timed joint velocity profiles drive the encoder/speed commands.

Encoder trials stay within 3° of the starting joints at 5% or less. Continuous mode samples the checked trajectory, pauses for tracking lag and stops at corners and the endpoint. Live encoders are verified; speed conversion is nominal.
